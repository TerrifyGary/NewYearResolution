# 嬰幼兒照顧與父母健康 / Early-life care and parental health

Status: requirements and initial source review, 2026-11-05. The v0.1 slice implements automatic care narration, agency gating and uncalibrated early mortality; detailed care/habit generation and production data below remain pending. See IMPLEMENTATION_STATUS.md.

## Confirmed behavior
Before player-agency onset, all feeding, vaccination, illness recognition and care decisions are made by guardian/NPC simulation. Narration and the journal describe outcomes without asking the infant player to choose. Routine care is scheduled by age in months, historical availability and individual circumstances; annual turns must not collapse infant care into one yearly roll.

Record breastfeeding/formula/mixed feeding, complementary-food introduction and nutritional adequacy, weaning separately, growth, vaccination product/doses/dates, missed appointments and follow-up. Complementary food does not automatically mean weaning. Adequate age-appropriate nutrition supports growth and health; do not treat every serving as a stackable immunity bonus or protection against a specific infection. Any health-point translation is a labeled gameplay estimate. Vaccines protect against their indicated diseases/types, not all illness; use historically valid schedules and contraindications rather than assuming all babies receive every vaccine.

Model infection exposure, infection, severity, symptom recognition, care access, treatment and recovery as separate stages. Overwork, caregiver fatigue, information, transport, money, support and disease presentation may influence recognition/access. New parents or low-income households are not automatically negligent, and severe illness can occur despite attentive care. Recognition delays can be a causal story branch, but quantitative effects need evidence or explicit estimated status. Disease progression uses finer-resolution probabilities, never natural-1 automatic death.

## Infant and child mortality — confirmed
Disease and accidental death from live birth onward are valid terminal outcomes, including before player-agency onset and before any annual goal has been chosen. Do not guarantee survival to childhood or adulthood, secretly reroll a fatal outcome, or suppress evidence-supported risk for player retention. Do not inflate rare risks for dramatic effect either.

Calibrate by applicable historical year, precise age band and supported health/exposure factors. Distinguish neonatal, post-neonatal and later-childhood denominators and time windows. Do not add cause-specific disease/accident hazards on top of an unchanged all-cause mortality draw; use a coherent competing-risk or reconciled baseline model. Exact rates and coefficients require sourced data and are not yet specified.

When death occurs, stop that character's simulation at the recorded date, cancel their future actions and agency activation, and preserve the causal event chain, care history, family identities and resolved costs. Display a life summary suitable for a life measured in days or months. Apply ordinary estate/legacy eligibility checks; an infant with no eligible living descendants ends the run and may start a new game. Do not invent a successor or revive the character to force continuation. This does not expand succession to siblings or parents.

Required future tests: a deterministic disease-death fixture and accident-death fixture before agency onset; no choice prompts or postmortem events; persisted death date/cause across save/load without rerolls; correct day/month age display; no duplicate all-cause/cause-specific mortality; final life summary and no-descendant termination. v0.1 now includes deterministic early-death and terminal-state tests; historical calibration and detailed care simulation remain pending.

## Parent generation and persistence
Generate each parent's existing condition history, diagnosis awareness, treatment/control, smoking status (never/former/current), exposure intensity/location, and alcohol frequency/quantity/binge indicators using supported year, age and sex strata. Include other guardians and household smokers in child exposure. Distinguish recent drinking from habitual heavy drinking and dependence. A parent's disease is not automatically inherited by the child.

Use joint or conditional estimates when available. Do not independently combine marginal smoking, alcohol and chronic-disease rates and describe the resulting household as an observed distribution. Start-of-run prevalence is not annual incidence: persist conditions and update initiation, cessation, onset and management through separate transition rules. Record source IDs, denominator, variable definition, survey years, uncertainty, fallback level, model version and seeded draws. Adult-population statistics are not directly representative of parents at childbirth.

## Environment
Derive outdoor exposure from residence history, season, historical monitoring coverage and suitable published environmental evidence. Record indoor secondhand-smoke exposure separately from whether a parent smokes; do not infer home exposure with certainty from smoking status. Avoid double counting correlated exposure risks. A district name, proximity to 六輕, low income or a parent's employer alone cannot determine a fixed health deduction. Local industrial exposure effects require appropriate historical/local evidence; missing evidence means an explicitly labeled estimate or unmodeled effect, not an invented measured penalty. Monitoring data are environmental measurements, not individual disease probabilities.

## Future acceptance requirements
No pre-agency care prompts; deterministic parent profiles; stable save/load; probabilities within bounds; matching denominators and survey years; smoking and alcohol definitions preserved; current vs former smoking distinguished; no annual reroll of prevalent disease; no vaccine before availability; complementary feeding distinct from weaning; no unbounded food health bonus; care-delay branches without income-based certainty; no location-only health penalty. Statistical calibration must compare generated cohorts to the actual source strata with uncertainty. These detailed acceptance requirements remain planned; the implemented subset and executed checks are listed in IMPLEMENTATION_STATUS.md.
