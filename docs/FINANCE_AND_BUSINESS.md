# 年度財務、社會保險、投資與創業

Status: confirmed scope and implementation requirements, 2026-11-05. The v0.1 slice implements a fictional basic ledger/job/closing model only; the detailed systems and official tables below remain pending. See IMPLEMENTATION_STATUS.md.

## Annual closing
Advertised salaries and compensation are gross, before income tax and employee deductions. Record employment periods, salary, bonuses and taxable/non-taxable benefits separately. Before the narrative year review, reconcile the financial year and show gross income, deductions, cash flow, assets, liabilities and net worth, alongside the fortune-slip narrative title. A good fortune title is not necessarily a profitable year and never changes monetary results.

Use dated ledger entries throughout the year, not a second deduction of everything in December. The year-end report totals already paid amounts, settles any genuinely outstanding amounts and records accrued obligations. Distinguish income year, liability period and payment date; preserve historical filing/payment timing rather than inventing December tax deadlines. Separate income-tax withholding, assessed tax, credits, refunds and tax payable. Repeatedly viewing or loading a closing report must not repeat any posting.

Apply relevant-year individual/family tax-unit rules and deductions. Track real estate (house tax, land value tax, ownership/use/local rules), vehicle license tax and fuel usage fees by eligible vehicle type and holding/registration periods; do not charge a renter ownership taxes or infer taxable values directly from market price. NHI and labor insurance premiums are insurance contributions, not taxes; fuel usage fees are a separate fee category. Also track living costs, education, care, maintenance, travel, loan interest/principal and medical expenses by their correct payer and category.

Cash reconciliation: opening cash + actual inflows − actual outflows = closing cash. Report investment purchases as cash-to-asset transfers, loan principal payments as liability reductions and employer retirement contributions as restricted pension assets, not ordinary consumption or spendable salary. Net worth = assets − liabilities, with valuation methods and limitations disclosed. Separate realized income/cash proceeds, unrealized market gains, business equity and restricted pension assets. Avoid double counting a business's assets both directly and in the owner's business equity.

## Labor insurance, NHI and retirement
Coverage is conditional on actual employment/insurance category and year, not universal for every adult. Maintain dated coverage periods, insured salary brackets, applicable rates, employee/employer/government shares, dependent NHI premiums and relevant supplemental premiums. Show annual employee-paid premiums and employer-funded benefits separately. Job loss, self-employment and incorporation can change category and eligibility; investigate other applicable schemes rather than leaving every character permanently on employee insurance.

Labor insurance old-age benefits (勞保老年給付) and labor retirement pensions (勞工退休金 / 勞退) are distinct. Model applicable old/new systems, insured years, contribution history, eligibility, benefit calculation, application, commencement, early/deferred claiming and benefit frequency using sourced historical rules. Retiring from work does not automatically confer pension eligibility. Employer-required contributions must not be deducted from the employee's gross salary; optional employee contributions are separate. Pension account balances and insurance entitlements cannot be treated as the same fund.

## Entrepreneurship stories
### Industry-specific employment risk and adult learning
Use historically supported industry/employer/time-specific separation risk, with distinct layoff, closure and contract-expiry events. A population unemployment rate measures a stock and must not be substituted for annual job-loss incidence. Public/private and employment-contract differences matter. Missing detailed rates must remain labeled estimates. Shared downturns can affect friends, employers and customer demand together; do not independently apply the same shock multiple times. A job loss dated within a year stops subsequent wages from that job, changes applicable insurance/benefit status, and enables realistic job seeking or retraining without forcing instant reemployment.

Post-graduation NPC relationships can unlock interest groups, mentoring, practice and new skills across the adult lifespan. Track specific proficiency and relevant experience separately from interest, friendship strength and the six core attributes. Interest encourages engagement; attended practice and instruction drive learning. Preserve the same time/resource budget across employment, caregiving, hobbies and entrepreneurship. No free skill points merely from gaining a friend.

### Specialized skills and business outcomes
Relevant proficiency must materially improve the matching parts of a business, not just narrative flavor. Coffee interest unlocks relevant learning and invitations; demonstrated coffee preparation/quality-control skill gives a substantial, explicitly labeled gameplay advantage in cafe product quality, consistency, waste control and customer retention. A beginner's interest alone is not equivalent to professional skill. Similar mappings include beverage preparation for drink shops, textile/equipment skills for laundry operations, and production/process skills for factories. Management, accounting, marketing and staff leadership are separate transferable proficiencies.

Advantages remain conditional on business model, staffing and the owner's actual contribution; a skilled hired worker or partner can supply missing expertise at a cost. Use proficiency in operational models and meaningful D20 sub-checks where appropriate. Do not guarantee annual profit from a hobby score: rent, debt, demand, competition, working capital and regulations still matter. Numeric weights, skill thresholds and business-specific success metrics remain uncalibrated gameplay parameters.

During working life, persistent friends/colleagues can propose entrepreneurship: a franchised beverage store, a cafe, a laundry business or a small factory. Invitations depend on NPC networks, interests and circumstances; do not force one every year. The player can decline, investigate or commit after eligibility, capital and time checks. Distinguish independent ownership, franchise, partnership and company organization; real brands/contracts only where historically verified, otherwise label fictional proposals.

Progression: invitation → feasibility/partner/contract assessment → funding and registration → premises/equipment/permits → opening → recurring operations → expansion, sale, closure or insolvency. Budgets include deposits, setup, franchise fees/royalties where applicable, working capital, rent, staffing, employer insurance/pension costs, supplies, utilities, repair, tax obligations and financing. Factory cases require suitable site, safety/environmental and regulatory constraints supported by applicable-year evidence.

Track sales, cost of goods, payroll, depreciation, operating profit, tax and cash separately. Sales are not personal income. Legal form determines business taxation, owner distributions and liability; do not tax the same profit twice through an invented universal rule. Demand, location, competition, management, economic cycles and capacity govern operating outcomes; D20 can resolve a meaningful negotiation or pitch but cannot stand in for an entire annual profit calculation. Stories include weak trade, rising rent/material costs, staff turnover, equipment failure, partner disputes, successful growth and realistic exit consequences. Owner work consumes time and can affect fatigue, family and other employment.

## Securities and annual investment choices
Age 20 is the user-specified gameplay unlock for opening a securities account, not a statement of statutory account-opening age. Historical legal and product eligibility must still be satisfied. Interpret “每年選擇三種投資” provisionally as up to three selected products/allocations per year, including the option to invest nothing; the exact product menu and risk classification require confirmation before implementation.

Use R1–R4 as provisional game risk bands, explicitly not an official universal classification or a guarantee of returns. Define each band's liquidity, volatility, loss, fees and underlying exposure before assigning products. Do not turn higher risk into guaranteed higher profit. Only offer products that existed and were accessible in the simulated year; no automatic borrowing or leverage. Validate purchase dates, available funds, units, transaction costs and sale settlement. Historical returns require sourced series, dividends and corporate actions without look-ahead; future returns are labeled seeded scenarios. Shared market factors make holdings correlated rather than three independent lucky draws.

At closing show contributions, withdrawals, distributions, realized profit/loss, unrealized profit/loss, fees/taxes, market value and cash separately. Apply actual-year tax rules to relevant income/transactions, not a blanket tax on all unrealized gains. Existing holdings remain until sold/matured rather than being automatically liquidated at December closing. Withdrawal/illiquidity constraints matter for emergencies and entrepreneurship funding.

## Domain and acceptance contract
Additional planned domain fields: employment separation reason/date and risk-source version; NPC learning opportunity, participation and proficiency changes; business-to-skill mappings and source of expertise (owner/partner/staff). Additional planned tests: industry-risk fixtures, correct salary cutoff and coverage transition after layoff, persistent adult friendships and earned skill growth, no skill transfer from mere acquaintance, shared time-budget conservation, and controlled cafe fixtures showing better operational quality with relevant proficiency while allowing losses from adverse market/cost conditions. These tests are not yet implemented.

Planned domain records: LedgerEntry, AnnualFinancialReport, TaxAssessment, InsuranceCoveragePeriod, PensionAccount, PensionEntitlement, BusinessEntity, BusinessOwnership, BusinessProposal, InvestmentAccount, InvestmentOrder, Holding and MarketScenario. Include stable IDs, currency/rounding, owner/payer, effective/payment dates, source/rule versions and saved seeded state.

Planned tests: ledger/cash/net-worth conservation; no double premium/tax/closing charges; employee vs employer retirement costs; midyear job/coverage changes; historical pension eligibility; tax accrual vs actual cash payment; property/vehicle applicability; business-personal transfers and tax treatment; no revenue-as-profit error; failed business and debt consequences; age-20 investment gate; three-allocation limit; insufficient-funds checks; correlated deterministic returns; dividend/fee/corporate-action accounting; no look-ahead; save migration and replay. UI must provide bilingual itemized closing statements and explain unavailable actions. v0.1 implements and tests the basic ledger/job/closing subset only; the full requirements above remain planned.
