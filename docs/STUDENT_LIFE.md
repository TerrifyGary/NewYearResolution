# Student identity, examination and placement — 2026-11-08

## Playable flow

1. Kindergarten is caregiver-arranged at age three, without a pre-agency choice. September changes 小班 → 中班 → 大班. The animal classroom name is deterministic.
2. Primary and junior admission retain general and specialty routes. Status displays school, grade, class and seat. Junior grades are 7–9. Music and sports still require the selected instrument/sport and D20 specialty entry checks.
3. Junior-high subjects are 國文、數學、英文、自然、社會, each on a separate 0–100 **game proficiency** scale. Annual study can target one subject (+7) or all five (+7 each); balanced development adds +3 each. Existing saves without subject detail begin with the previous aggregate academic value for all five; this is a compatibility baseline, not reconstructed exam history.
4. Ordinary post-junior admission: five subject D20 checks → show the total → choose 高中 / 高職 / 五專 → for vocational/college, choose a department → placement → acceptance notice. Choosing a route/department never rerolls the saved exam. Music/sports specialty entry remains an independent alternative.
5. For each ordinary subject, total = D20 + floor((INT−10)/2) + floor(subject proficiency/15). Five totals are summed. Individual pass labels use fictional DC12 for feedback; **placement uses the sum**, not a requirement to pass all five subjects.
6. Senior-high candidates are filtered by the user-defined geographic rule (Taipei/New Taipei reciprocal; other cities local) and ordinary-route gender eligibility. Placement takes the highest fictional qualifying threshold, ties by stable school ID. Current game thresholds are 60 points for ordinary catalog schools and 90 for the newly added 建國高中/北一女中. These are not CAP points, real school rankings, official cutoffs or a reproduction of Taiwan admission law. Both newly added identities have conservative coverage from 2026. Their ordinary route is offered here; science-class or other gender/route exceptions are not modeled.
7. 高職 departments: 商管科 / 美顏科 / 資訊科. 五專 adds 護理科. Current minimum totals are fictional 40/50 respectively. Actual vocational/college school catalogs are not ingested, so placement explicitly names a **所在地模擬高職／五專**, not an invented real institution. The five-year course displays 專1–5; vocational high displays 高職1–3.
8. Senior high displays 高1／高一 through 高3／高三 and class number. An August25 (illustrative) story before year two selects humanities, science/engineering or life/health sciences. This simplified group is persisted and does not lock unrelated admission routes.
9. University/master entry includes a department-selection story. Departments are labeled simulated pending verification of actual school/year offerings. University displays 大1–4; master displays 碩1–4.
10. From the end of master year two, a June15 (illustrative) thesis/supervisor D20 INT/academic check at fictional DC15 can award a degree. Failure permits another year; after the fourth unsuccessful/deferred review, the pilot records leaving without a degree. Four years is a gameplay boundary, not an assertion of legal maximum study duration. There is no automatic degree merely for reaching year four.
11. Successful admission/hiring shows a dismissible bilingual 「恭喜錄取 XXXX」 notice. Caregiver-arranged kindergarten gets the same notification. Declining a master route, deferring a job, or failed placement does not produce congratulations. Compulsory general-school fallback may be congratulated, but is not specialty acceptance.
12. New hires record fictional company, role and start year. Status displays work year = current year − start year + 1. Missing older employment dates remain unrecorded instead of inventing tenure. Active school status takes priority over employment status.

## Schools and generated details

Known local school names are selected from the source catalog and supported years. A known cluster is preferred when moving stages; this is not verified street-address catchment allocation. Missing data uses a stable random local simulated-school name. No measured distance or guaranteed right of attendance is claimed. Kindergarten's current pilot locations lack a full sourced catalog. Animal classes, class sizes/numbers and seats are fictional; deterministic identity hashes make reloads stable without consuming the simulation RNG. An actual trio such as 東湖國小附幼／東湖國小／東湖國中 is a future catalog expansion, not currently assigned to unrelated birth districts.

## Persistence and engineering

The engine owns school identity/status, subjects, five dice, pending route selection, placement, groups, degrees, employment and notification receipts. UI rendering consumes no RNG. Optional additive schema3 fields store subject detail, exam history, pending admission, enrollment metadata/outcome, employment and the notification. Existing v1/v2 migration and older schema3 saves still load without inventing old departments, employment dates or degrees. New progression applies to subsequent actions; historical ledger/journal entries are retained. The changed master progression and future ordinary-admission sequence are new gameplay behavior, not a promise of replaying old commands under identical old rules.

Saved exam arrays validate each die and modifier arithmetic, their five-result length and sum. Pending totals remain tied to their simulation year. Descendant transfer clears the previous player's pending placement/notice. Each resolved action is charged/rolled once. No national school catalog, official historical CAP conversion, real placement quota system or Flutter implementation is claimed.
